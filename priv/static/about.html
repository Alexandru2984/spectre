LESS FOREVER. MORE NOW.

A small space for
passing thoughts.

Some thoughts don’t need a profile, an algorithm, or a permanent home. They just need a moment. Spectre is a small public space for those thoughts.

Leave a thought. Meet the moment.

Choose a room, write a thought, idea or question, and pick how long it stays: 15 seconds, 30 seconds, one minute or five minutes. Everyone in that public room can read and react while it’s here.

A few ways to be here

  • Find your corner in The lobby, Bright ideas, Open questions or After hours.
  • Open a named public room and share the link. No signup or room reservation.
  • Explore the live feed or switch to the interactive constellation.
  • Find a thought with search, filters and sorting. React with inspiration, love or curiosity.
  • Burn your own message before its timer runs out, using the control kept in that browser tab.

Designed for the moment

Messages live in application memory and disappear from the board when their timer expires or the service restarts. There is no user account, permanent message history, attachment upload or message export archive.

Public means public

A room name is not a password. Anyone who knows or guesses it can read that room. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, and someone can copy or screenshot them. Please share thoughts, never secrets. Read the privacy and retention note before posting sensitive information.

A little easier to use

Spectre works on mobile and desktop, with light and dark themes, keyboard shortcuts, reduced motion, and an accessible feed alongside the canvas. Press N to write, / to search, or ? for a quick guide.
