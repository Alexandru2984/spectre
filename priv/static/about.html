LESS FOREVER. MORE NOW.

A small space for
passing thoughts.

Some thoughts don’t need a profile, an algorithm, or a permanent home. They just need a moment. Spectre is a small public space for those thoughts.

Conversations that leave room for the moment.

Share a thought, idea, question or poll. Reply in a short thread, react, or vote. Every message lasts at most five minutes; replies disappear no later than their parent.

Your event, your little corner.

Create a 15, 30 or 60 minute event with a topic. Choose public or invitation-only access, and explicitly opt a public room into discovery. Share an invitation link or locally generated QR code. Owners can close their event early and moderate its messages.

Here together, without a profile.

See active page sessions and optional temporary aliases. Changes arrive through bounded live connections. Browse the feed or constellation, try the daily prompt, search and filter, and choose Romanian or English. Aliases, votes and session counts are not verified identities.

Private access and optional encrypted text.

Public rooms are readable by everyone. Private event rooms require an invitation. Optional end-to-end encryption uses your browser to encrypt message, reply and poll text before sending it. The server still sees metadata such as room topic, timing, aliases, message kinds and vote counts.

Care for the space.

Hide a message locally, report spam or harassment, and let an authorized moderator review active reports. Reports expire with the message and do not automatically remove it. Closing an event removes its messages, polls, reports and sessions.

No accounts or content database are required. Appearance and language preferences may be saved in your browser; message bodies, invitations and owner keys are kept only in page memory. Reloading loses unsaved controls.
