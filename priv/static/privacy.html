LESS FOREVER. MORE NOW.

A clear note
on privacy.

Ephemeral describes the lifetime of a message on this board. It is not a promise of secrecy, anonymity against every observer, or physical erasure of every copy.

Updated 5 November 2026

All rooms are public

Anyone who knows or guesses a room name can read and react to its messages. Room links grant no private access. Do not post passwords, personal records, financial details or other secrets. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

What is held in application memory

Active message text, room name, category, timestamps and reactions are kept in the server process until expiry or restart. Message lifetime is at most five minutes. Independent timers remove expired records even when no one reads the board. Restarting the service drops active messages.

The creator receives a random burn key. Only its hash is kept by the server; presenting the key can remove that message early. Reaction tokens are random per page load and stored as hashes with active messages. Anonymous rate limits use short-lived hashed client address keys; they are not user accounts or a guarantee of unlinkability.

What your browser keeps

Message text, your draft, reaction choices and burn controls are held in the page’s memory. They are not written to localStorage, sessionStorage or a service-worker cache by this app. Only theme, preferred view and motion preferences may be saved in localStorage. Reloading or closing the page loses burn controls.

Copies and infrastructure

Other people can copy, save or screenshot a message. Browser memory, network intermediaries, system memory, swap and backups outside this app may retain data beyond the visible timer. This app cannot revoke those copies or promise secure physical erasure.

Traffic passes through Cloudflare and the server’s nginx proxy. The application’s nginx access log omits client IP, query strings, bodies, cookies and burn headers. Error logs and upstream services may retain additional operational data under the operator’s existing policies. This page makes no claim that the entire hosting infrastructure is log-free.

No account or advertising profile

The app does not require an account and adds no analytics or advertising script. Transport encryption protects HTTPS traffic in transit; the server still processes message text. Room names and text are not proof of a participant’s identity.

Search indexing

Only the public homepage and informational pages are listed in the sitemap. Room URLs and API responses receive noindex headers; messages are not rendered into public page metadata or social previews. Search-engine directives are not access control.
